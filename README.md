# Hi, I'm Jason Calalang

I'm an enterprise architect who still does the engineering. I work as an independent consultant, and I'm CTO of Ridgewell Management Services.

Most of my 22-plus years in enterprise IT were spent at IBM, on banking and telecommunications projects in the Philippines and abroad.

## What I work on

- Hybrid cloud and platform architecture
- Kubernetes and OpenShift
- Enterprise integration and middleware
- DevOps, infrastructure as code, and keeping systems reliable once they're in production
- Agile delivery with the IBM Garage method, Scrum, and Kanban
